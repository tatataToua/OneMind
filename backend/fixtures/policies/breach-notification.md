# Breach Notification

**Source:** 45 CFR 164.400-414 — paraphrased for internal guidance.
**Owner:** Privacy Office | **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-11

A breach is an acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of PHI not permitted by
the Privacy Rule that compromises its security or privacy. An impermissible use
or disclosure is **presumed** to be a breach unless a risk assessment shows a low
probability that PHI has been compromised.

## Risk assessment factors

1. The nature and extent of the PHI involved, including the identifiers present
   and the likelihood of re-identification.
2. The unauthorised person who used the PHI, or to whom the disclosure was made.
3. Whether the PHI was actually acquired or viewed.
4. The extent to which the risk to the PHI has been mitigated.

## Timelines

Notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and no later than 60
calendar days after discovery. Breaches affecting 500 or more individuals require
notice to the Secretary and to prominent media outlets in the affected
jurisdiction within the same 60-day window. Smaller breaches may be logged and
reported annually, no later than 60 days after the end of the calendar year.

Encryption meeting the Guidance Specifying the Technologies and Methodologies
renders PHI unusable, and unauthorised access to properly encrypted data is
therefore not a reportable breach.
